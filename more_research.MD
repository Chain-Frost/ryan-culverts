## 1. Executive conclusion

**Phase 0 cannot be closed.** Material parts of CS-001 were resolved, but the required full-primary-source gate is not satisfied. In particular, the full applicable Austroads material, NCHRP Report 734, the HY-8 8.0.1.2 technical/user manual, and the complete HDS-5 publication were not all successfully accessed and reviewed in this research pass. FHWA identifies HDS-5 Third Edition as FHWA-HIF-12-026 and makes the 49 MB PDF available, but the complete PDF could not be loaded in the available retrieval environment. citeturn34search0turn34search5

The principal conclusions are:

- **The current rule `tailwater_depth >= culvert_rise => whole barrel is full flow is not a defensible universal regime rule.** It establishes that the *outlet cross-section is submerged*, not that the hydraulic grade line remains above the crown for the entire barrel. A mixed free-surface/pressurised solution or a supercritical-to-subcritical transition may exist. HEC-RAS explicitly models supercritical and mixed culvert profiles rather than reducing a submerged outlet to whole-barrel full flow. The repository presently makes the shortcut both in `solver/regime.py` and `profiles/direct_step.py`; this is the highest-priority hydraulic defect. [SRC-HECRAS-7, mixed-flow section]; repository lines cited in §5.
- **The current inlet transition is project-defined, not FHWA-prescribed.** HDS-5 describes constructing the transition by joining the unsubmerged and submerged inlet-control relationships with a smooth curve/line tangent to both; the accessible HDS-5 material does not prescribe linear interpolation in `q*`. A searchable copy of the HDS-5 transition discussion likewise describes a line tangent to the two curves. citeturn34search17 The repository explicitly performs linear interpolation from `q*=3.5` to `4.0`.
- **Bodhaine's six flow types are not equivalent to the repository's current `FlowRegime` enum.** Bodhaine distinguishes control location and barrel state: critical control at inlet, critical control at outlet, tranquil backwater, submerged full flow, high-head part-full flow, and high-head full flow with free outfall. Types 5 and 6 in particular show why “inlet submerged”, “outlet submerged”, “full flow” and “inlet/outlet control” must remain separate hydraulic facts. Bodhaine also says the Type 5/6 boundary can be difficult to predict and depends on entrance geometry, barrel length, slope and roughness. citeturn35view4turn35view5
- **FHWA-HRT-06-138 must be used only in its corrected electronic form.** Its errata replace Tables 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 17 and 18. The research is an expansion/refinement for specified rectangular-box inlet geometries; it is not authority to replace every HDS-5 box coefficient indiscriminately. The current repository's conventional HDS-5 Chart 8/10 constants should therefore not be globally replaced.
- **MRWA hydraulic roughness lookup and MRWA construction compliance are distinct.** Current MRWA hydraulic guidance contains helically wound CSP `n` values for combinations that current Specification 404 does not permit as its standard spirally wound CSP products. Specification 404 Issue 17, July 2026 permits 68 × 13 mm corrugations through 1500 mm and 125 × 25 mm for 1650–2100 mm under clause 404.07.02; the hydraulic roughness table contains additional diameters and 75 × 25 mm combinations. Those datasets must not be represented by one “available” boolean.
- **The current silent inlet-geometry defaults are unsafe as engineering defaults.** A circular concrete barrel currently becomes “square edge with headwall”, CSP becomes “headwall”, and a concrete box becomes “30°–75° flared wingwalls” unless coefficients are supplied. Those are physical inlet configurations, not properties inferable from shape/material. They should be resolved through the requested provenance hierarchy or rejected.
- **HY-8 8.0.1.2 is the comparison version.** FHWA's current release page identifies 8.0.1.2, build date 2025-03-05. citeturn32search0 The `run-hy8` repository contains useful read-only evidence from an installed 8.0.1.2 system, including observed `ShapeDB.dat` roughness values and contextual inlet lists, but this evidence is secondary because the HY-8 manual itself was not directly reviewed in this pass.
- **Recommended implementation order:** first remove/fail-close the submerged-tailwater/full-barrel shortcut; then replace the inlet-transition policy; then implement the provenance-aware defaults resolver and separate hydraulic availability from Spec 404 compliance; then implement/validate the profile-state machine and hydraulic jumps; only after that should combined regime selection, external validation, rating curves and performance optimisation be accepted.

No hydraulic Python, tests, package versions or existing documentation were changed. No commit, staging operation, package publication or other publication was made.
